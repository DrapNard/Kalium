# Kalium — Artist-First Charter

Kalium exists to put sophisticated 3D texture painting back in artists' hands.

1. **Human creative agency.** Features empower artists to make artistic decisions. The official app does not include prompt-to-texture, generative image, generative asset, or generative material features.
2. **Tools, not replacements.** Normal/curvature/AO baking, noise nodes, procedural materials, selection helpers, UV seam repair, deterministic texture processing and accessibility tools are encouraged.
3. **Code assistance is allowed.** Contributors may use AI-assisted coding with review, tests, licensing provenance, and maintainable implementations. Generated artwork is not accepted as default project art.
4. **No account or online dependency.** Projects must load/save locally; existing art must never be held hostage by a service.
5. **Maximum interoperability.** Provide open formats and independently developed importers for legally possessed third-party formats.
6. **No telemetry, subscriptions or dark patterns.** Processing occurs on the user's machine by default.
7. **Be honest about compatibility.** Never describe a signature detector, thumbnail extractor or flattened image importer as editable Substance Painter project support.

## Relationship to AGPL

The official distribution follows this charter and enforces it through maintainership and contributor review. **AGPLv3 does not permit prohibiting other people from modifying the software to add AI features.** Forks are allowed as required by the license. A separate trademark policy may distinguish official releases from forks; don't misuse copyright licensing to restrict fields of use.
